# Furrget

**Remember what was said, not what you assumed.**

Furrget records a meeting on your Mac and writes the minutes on your own template, where every point traces back to
the moment it was said. Meetings can mix Indonesian and English.

- **Download:** [Furrget for Mac](https://github.com/furrgetlabs/furrget-releases/releases) (Apple silicon, macOS 14.2 or later; Windows follows later)
- **Guide:** [furrget.app/docs](https://furrget.app/docs/)
- **Website:** [furrget.app](https://furrget.app)
- **Support:** support@furrget.app

Furrget is in beta. The app's source code is private; builds and the update feed are public in
[furrget-releases](https://github.com/furrgetlabs/furrget-releases).
